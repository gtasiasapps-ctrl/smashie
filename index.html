Our Story

No shortcuts. No frozen patties. No compromise.

Smashie was born in a tiny Athens kitchen with a cast-iron pan and a dream. We spent two years perfecting the smash — the right fat ratio, the perfect sear, the bun that holds without crumbling. Today, we serve the finest smashed burgers in Greece.

From our griddle to your soul.
